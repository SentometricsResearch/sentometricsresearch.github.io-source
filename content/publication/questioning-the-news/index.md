Rather than committing to one way of turning news into a sentiment index, the paper generates thousands of candidate indices and lets a sparse regression select the ones that forecast. Winner of the *International Journal of Forecasting* best paper award 2018–2019.

The daily topical sentiment indices are [available for download](https://sentometrics-research.com/download/us-econ/).
