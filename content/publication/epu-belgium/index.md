The index is derived daily from major Belgian newspapers in French and Dutch since 2001, using triplets of economy / policy / uncertainty keywords defined by a combination of expert judgment and NLP. A 2021 revision made the index more local to Belgium and better aligned across the two languages.

[Download the index](https://sentometrics-research.com/download/epu-belgium/).
