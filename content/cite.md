Everything published here is free to use for academic research. In exchange we ask two things: cite
the reference below, and put `https://sentometrics-research.com` in a footnote so others can find
the data. Full terms are in the [licence](https://www.dropbox.com/s/jwjh4b08zvq09nv/LICENSE.txt?dl=0).

## Indices

### Media Climate Change Concerns (MCCC)

```bibtex
@article{ArdiaBluteauBoudtInghelbrecht2023,
  author  = {Ardia, David and Bluteau, Keven and Boudt, Kris and Inghelbrecht, Koen},
  title   = {Climate change concerns and the performance of green versus brown stocks},
  journal = {Management Science},
  year    = {2023},
  doi     = {10.1287/mnsc.2022.4636}
}
```

## Methodology

If you are citing the field rather than one of its products, cite the survey:

```bibtex
@article{AlgabaArdiaBluteauBormsBoudt2020,
  author  = {Algaba, Andres and Ardia, David and Bluteau, Keven and Borms, Samuel and Boudt, Kris},
  title   = {Econometrics meets sentiment: An overview of methodology and applications},
  journal = {Journal of Economic Surveys},
  volume  = {34},
  pages   = {512--547},
  year    = {2020},
  doi     = {10.1111/joes.12370}
}
```
