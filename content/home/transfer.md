Everything here is built to be reused. The indices are free for academic research, the packages are
on CRAN and GitHub, and the methodology is documented in open-access papers.

- **[How to cite](https://sentometrics-research.com/cite/)** — ready-to-copy BibTeX for every index, package and method paper.
- **[Working on sentometrics](https://sentometrics-research.com/join/)** — where to start, which data to build on, and the topics we think are worth a thesis.
- **Using one of our indices?** [Tell us](https://github.com/SentometricsResearch/) — we keep a public record of the research they support.
