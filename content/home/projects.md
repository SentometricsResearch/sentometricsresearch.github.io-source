  <article class="project">
    <h3>Climate concern, priced</h3>
    <p class="lead">Concern about climate change cannot be observed, so it has to be measured.
    We built a daily index from the US press — risk and sentiment in every climate article,
    weighted by how much attention the media were paying — and showed that green stocks
    outperform brown ones when that concern rises unexpectedly.</p>
    <ul class="facts">
      <li><strong>2003 to 2025</strong>, updated every year</li>
      <li><em>Management Science</em> · National Bank of Belgium research award</li>
      <li>Covered by <em>The New York Times</em>; four master's theses built on it</li>
    </ul>
    <p class="links">
      <a href="https://sentometrics-research.com/download/mccc/">The MCCC index</a>
      <a href="https://sentometrics-research.com/publication/climate-change-concerns/">The paper</a>
    </p>
  </article>

  <article class="project">
    <h3>A century of uncertainty</h3>
    <p class="lead">Newspaper archives turned into macroeconomic indicators. A hundred years of
    economic policy uncertainty for Quebec, read through French-Canadian sources that the
    existing Canadian index had missed — and a daily bilingual index for Belgium.</p>
    <ul class="facts">
      <li><strong>1913 to 2020</strong> for Quebec, monthly</li>
      <li>Built with Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec and Radio-Canada</li>
      <li>Nowcasts Canadian and Quebec macro variables better than the existing EPU</li>
    </ul>
    <p class="links">
      <a href="https://sentometrics-research.com/download/epu-quebec/">EPU Quebec</a>
      <a href="https://sentometrics-research.com/download/epu-belgium/">EPU Belgium</a>
    </p>
  </article>

  <article class="project">
    <h3>The method, and the tools</h3>
    <p class="lead">Sentiment can be computed in many ways and pooled into a time series in many
    more. We treated that multiplicity as the problem to solve rather than a nuisance: define the
    field, then ship the software that lets anyone work in it.</p>
    <ul class="facts">
      <li>The survey that defines sentometrics, <em>Journal of Economic Surveys</em></li>
      <li><strong>sentometrics</strong> on CRAN, documented in the <em>Journal of Statistical Software</em></li>
      <li>Best paper 2018–2019, <em>International Journal of Forecasting</em></li>
    </ul>
    <p class="links">
      <a href="https://sentometrics-research.com/publication/econometrics-meets-sentiment/">The survey</a>
      <a href="https://sentometrics-research.com/publication/sentometrics-r-package/">The package</a>
    </p>
